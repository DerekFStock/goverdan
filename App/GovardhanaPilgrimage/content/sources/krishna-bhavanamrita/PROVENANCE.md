# Kṛṣṇa-bhāvanāmṛta English supplied-witness edition

The private reader package was mechanically extracted from `Krishna Bhavanamrta.doc`, an HTML document saved with a `.doc` extension. The source is decoded as Windows-1252, then normalized to Unicode NFC with duplicated whitespace and empty Word paragraphs removed. English wording, spelling, names, bracketed explanations, speeches, and songs remain as supplied.

The witness does not identify its translator and exposes no canonical Sanskrit verse numbering. Runtime paragraph anchors such as `kba.ch09.p001` are stable app-reader locators, not claims about Sanskrit verse divisions. Sanskrit invocation stanzas and the Sanskrit quotation in the preface are omitted from the English-only edition; their supplied English translations remain. The translator's preface is marked as front matter. The final colophon and Cast of Characters are kept separate from chapter 20.

The exact source and extraction guard are implemented in `tools/import_krishna_bhavanamrita.py`. Its expected snapshot is twenty ordered chapters and 712 chapter-body passages. The chapter-six `End of Pratah Lila` cycle marker appears after that chapter's colophon in the source and is retained as the approved 59th reader passage for the chapter.
