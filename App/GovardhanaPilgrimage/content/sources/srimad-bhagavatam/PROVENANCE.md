# Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.36.1–16 — Development Provenance

## Scope and identity

This package contains the canonical project loci `10.36.1` through `10.36.16` for `work.srimad-bhagavatam`, following the controlled Vedabase/BBT numbering. It is a normalized project reading for development use, not a claim to establish a critical edition of the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.

Verses 1–15 narrate Ariṣṭāsura’s entry, attack, defeat, and death. Verse 16 transitions to Nārada’s address to Kaṁsa. The root text of verse 16 does not narrate the manifestation of the two kuṇḍas.

## Controlling reading witness

The Sanskrit and IAST were directly checked on 2026-08-28 against the Bhaktivedanta Vedabase pages:

- Chapter: <https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/10/36/>
- Verses 1–2 and 5–16: `https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/10/36/{verse}/`
- Combined segmentation page for verses 3–4: <https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/10/36/3-4/>

The project preserves separate canonical loci for 10.36.3 and 10.36.4 while recording that the controlling web witness presents them on a combined page. If another Sanskrit witness uses different segmentation or numbering, that difference must be recorded and reviewed; it must not silently alter these controlled canonical IDs.

## Translation

The English renderings are fresh literal Govardhana Pilgrimage Project development translations made from the Sanskrit. They do not reproduce the copyrighted Vedabase/BBT English translations. Every passage remains `translation_status: WORKING_PROJECT`; none is APP_READY or approved for release.

## Negative finding and related Work

Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.36 narrates Ariṣṭāsura’s attack and death, but the root verses do not narrate Rādhā’s playful accusation, Kṛṣṇa’s summoning of the tīrthas, the manifestation of Kṛṣṇa-kuṇḍa and Rādhā-kuṇḍa, the joining of their waters, or the reciprocal declarations.

The Vedabase commentary at 10.36.16 preserves/transmits a separate twenty-verse account in connection with Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura’s commentary. The application retains that account as the independent `work.radhakunda-manifestation-puranic-unit`, with its exact underlying Purāṇa and recension unresolved.
