# Govinda-līlāmṛta 5.78 — Lalitā episode provenance

Only fifth sarga, verse 78 is included in the Task 022 development package. The exact Sanskrit locus and wording were checked in the project's local *Govinda-līlāmṛtam (mūla-mātram)* DOCX and independently in the *Govinda-līlāmṛta* DOCX with Sadānanda-vidhāyinī commentary, fifth sarga, verse 78. The commentary gloss explicitly states Lalitā's instruction to Tulasī, Vṛndā's learning of the appointed play-grove from Subala, and Tulasī's departure with Kastūrī, garland, and betel. The mūla transcription joins `kastūryālī`, while the commentary witness segments `kastūry-ālī`; the project reading follows the latter segmentation and does not treat it as a substantive variant.

The compiled English is the existing concise research rendering from *RK-03_Radhakunda_People_of_the_Landscape_Research_Ledger_v0.3.docx*, Tulasī entry. It is marked `WORKING_PROJECT`, because it is not a full literal translation and has not undergone publication editorial approval. The article's short retelling is limited to actions directly supported by the verse and gloss. No motive, modern coordinate, or additional līlā action is inferred.
