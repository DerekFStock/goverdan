# TASK 021A.8 — iPhone field checklist

Use only public, safe ground. Keep iPhone location accuracy, capture time, direction of approach, and photographer/source for each GPS sample and photo. Do not enter a restricted compound or step into/over water for evidence. A water edge is **not** an arrival point.

## `RK-03` Saṅgama crossing

- From safe ground, photograph the whole crossing and both ends, plus any signs or steps. Copy exact sign text.
- Record a GPS sample at each publicly accessible land-side end; note accuracy, barriers, current walkability, and whether it is a bridge, stone path, or another structure.
- Determine whether “Saṅgama Ghāṭ” (`RK-GH-07`) names the crossing itself, one/both bank approaches, or another adjacent feature. Do not combine the two lake outlines.

## `RK-15` Girirāja-jihvā

- Photograph the temple exterior and exact name sign from the public way; confirm this is Jihvā-śilā, not merely Gopī-kūpa or another Govardhana-śilā site.
- Record GPS and accuracy at the actual public doorway. Note whether the śilā is inside a temple/compound and whether that doorway is routinely open to pilgrims.
- Ask the operator about opening and photography restrictions; do not photograph inside without permission.

## `RK-22` Mahāprabhu baithak

- Photograph exact sign text and a wide view showing platform, tree, footprints, temple, and any enclosing compound, from public ground.
- Confirm whether this is Caitanya Mahāprabhu's Tamala Tala, not the Vallabhācārya “Mahāprabhuji Baithakji” namesake. Record operator or caretaker identification if offered.
- Record GPS/accuracy at a safe public entrance or approach and note access restrictions. Sketch which named features are inside one parent site; do not infer the tree's age or historical continuity.

## `RK-GH-09` Mānasa-pāvana Ghāṭ

- From a safe public path, photograph the bank/steps, any sandstone name inscription, and their relation to pīlu and Pañca-Pāṇḍava trees or markers.
- Record GPS/accuracy only at a dry-land public approach; separately identify the named edge or steps without using a water coordinate for arrival.
- Ask whether `RK-13` and `RK-GH-10` name parts of this ghāṭ, adjacent sites, or distinct traditions; record exact sign wording and public access. Avoid unsafe water positioning.
