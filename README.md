# Govardhana Pilgrimage

A scholarly and devotional research project centered on the sacred places and līlās of Govardhana and Rādhā-kuṇḍa.

The project distinguishes primary scripture and Gosvāmī sources, early Vraja pilgrimage tradition, and later or local tradition. Current work is focused on completing the Rādhā-kuṇḍa research foundation before writing the narrative guide.

## Repository contents

- Current research dossiers and ledgers
- Verified quotation and theological control documents
- Source books and archival references
- Earlier document versions in `archive/`
- Guide-development materials in `guide/`
